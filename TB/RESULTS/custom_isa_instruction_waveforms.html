Beginner Guide: How to Read These Waveforms

1. Time flows left to right

The bottom axis is simulation time. Each vertical grid line is a point in time.

2. Digital signals

A high line means logic 1. A low line means logic 0. Short pulses often mean one-cycle events.

3. Bus signals

Purple bus lines show multi-bit values such as PC, instruction, data, address, or ciphertext. Labels are hexadecimal.

4. Pipeline order

Instructions move IF to ID to EX to MEM to WB. Final register writes happen when WB reg_write is high.

5. MMIO peripherals

Peripheral select signals show which block is accessed: AES, sensor/SPI, UART, interrupt controller, DMA, or power block.

6. Custom ISA

custom ID, custom EX, and custom MEM show a custom instruction moving through the pipeline. custom cmd selects the security operation.
